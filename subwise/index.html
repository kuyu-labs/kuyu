Privacy Policy & Legal Disclosures

Subwise: Subscription Tracker • Last Updated: November 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes the policies and practices of Subwise: Subscription Tracker ("we", "our", or "the App") developed by Kuyu Labs regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use our mobile application.

100% Offline-First Architecture: Subwise does not operate remote database servers, does not transmit your personal financial records over the internet, and does not require creating an external account to track your subscriptions.

1. Data Collection Policy: Zero Remote Collection

We believe personal finance and recurring subscription information should remain strictly private. The Subwise application is designed as a standalone, on-device utility:

  • No Personal Financial Records Collected: All subscription names, costs, currencies, cycle frequencies, renewal dates, and payment history notes are stored exclusively on your device in a secure local database (SQLite/Room). This data is never sent to us or any third party.
  • No Account Creation: You do not need to register with an email, phone number, or password. You retain full, sole ownership of your information.
  • No Advertising SDKs: Subwise contains no third-party ad networks (such as Google AdMob, Unity Ads, or Meta Audience Network) and does not track your activity across other applications or websites.
  • No Third-Party Analytics: We do not integrate tracking frameworks (such as Firebase Analytics, Flurry, or Mixpanel). We do not collect device fingerprints, advertising IDs (GAID), or location data.

2. In-App Purchases & Google Play Billing

Subwise offers an optional one-time purchase ("Apex Pro") to unlock premium features, such as custom theme presets and automated calendar synchronization:

  • All payments, billing information, and transactions are processed directly and securely by Google Play (Google LLC) via the Google Play Billing Library.
  • Subwise never collects, sees, or stores your credit card numbers, bank accounts, or billing addresses.
  • Purchase receipts are verified on-device via Google Play Store services in accordance with the Google Privacy Policy and Google Play Terms of Service.

3. Device Permissions & How They Are Used

Subwise only requests permissions strictly necessary to perform functions you explicitly choose to enable:

  • Notifications & Exact Alarms (POST_NOTIFICATIONS, SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM): Used solely to trigger scheduled, local on-device alarms reminding you when a subscription or bill is due. No notification content is transmitted over the internet.
  • Calendar Access (READ_CALENDAR, WRITE_CALENDAR - Optional): If you choose to enable calendar synchronization, the app uses this permission solely to create and update subscription reminder events on your local device calendar. We do not read your personal calendar entries, nor do we upload calendar data.
  • Local Backup & Storage Access: When exporting or importing JSON backups, the app interacts solely with the file location you choose via the Android system file picker (Storage Access Framework).

5. Third-Party Service Providers

Because Subwise does not store or analyze your data on remote servers, we do not employ external service providers to process personal user information. The only external system involved is Google Play for processing optional in-app purchases and providing app updates through the official store.

6. Data Retention, Portability, and Deletion

Because all data is stored locally on your device:

  • Data Portability: You can export a comprehensive copy of your subscriptions at any time via the Settings → Export Backup (JSON) feature.
  • Deletion: You can permanently erase all stored data by clearing the app data in Android Settings (Settings → Apps → Subwise → Storage → Clear Storage) or by uninstalling the application from your device. Since we do not host your data on external servers, no remote account deletion request is required.
