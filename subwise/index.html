Privacy Policy & Legal Disclosures

Subscription Tracker: Apex • Last Updated: November 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes the policies and practices of Subscription Tracker: Apex ("we", "our", or "the App") developed by Kuyu Labs regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use our mobile application.

Privacy-First On-Device Architecture: Subscription Tracker: Apex stores 100% of your personal financial records, subscription names, renewal dates, and costs strictly on your local device. We do not require account registration, and your financial entries are never transmitted to external servers.

1. Data Collection & Privacy Practices

We believe personal finance and recurring subscription information should remain strictly confidential. The Subscription Tracker: Apex application adheres to the following privacy standards:

  • 100% Local On-Device Financial Storage: All subscription details, costs, payment history notes, and renewal cycles are stored exclusively on your device in a private local database (SQLite/Room). We never collect, transmit, or have access to your personal financial records.
  • No Account Creation or Identity Linking: You are not required to create an account or provide your name, email, or phone number to use the app.
  • No Advertising Networks: The app is completely ad-free. It contains no ad SDKs (such as AdMob or Meta Ads), does not track you across other applications, and removes the Google Advertising ID (GAID) permission entirely.
  • Anonymous Aggregate Usage Analytics (Opt-Out Available): To diagnose crashes and improve app features (such as measuring the popularity of themes or backup utilities), we use Google Firebase Analytics to collect strictly anonymized, aggregate interaction metrics. We do not link these events to your personal identity, and no financial data or subscription names are ever included. You can easily disable analytics collection at any time in the app under Settings → Share Anonymous Usage Data.

2. In-App Purchases & Google Play Billing

Subscription Tracker: Apex offers optional one-time supporter purchases ("Supporter Mode" tiers) to unlock all features, such as custom theme presets, unlimited tracking, and automated calendar synchronization:

  • All payments, billing information, and transactions are processed directly and securely by Google Play (Google LLC) via the Google Play Billing Library.
  • Subscription Tracker: Apex never collects, sees, or stores your credit card numbers, bank accounts, or billing addresses.
  • Purchase receipts are verified on-device via Google Play Store services in accordance with the Google Privacy Policy and Google Play Terms of Service.

3. Device Permissions & How They Are Used

Subscription Tracker: Apex only requests permissions strictly necessary to perform functions you explicitly choose to enable:

  • Notifications & Exact Alarms (POST_NOTIFICATIONS, SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM): Used solely to trigger scheduled, local on-device alarms reminding you when a subscription or bill is due. No notification content is transmitted over the internet.
  • Calendar Access (READ_CALENDAR, WRITE_CALENDAR - Optional): If you choose to enable calendar synchronization, the app uses this permission solely to create and update subscription reminder events on your local device calendar. We do not read your personal calendar entries, nor do we upload calendar data.
  • Local Backup & Storage Access: When exporting or importing JSON backups, the app interacts solely with the file location you choose via the Android system file picker (Storage Access Framework).

6. Data Retention, Portability, and Deletion

Because all data is stored locally on your device:

  • Data Portability: You can export a comprehensive copy of your subscriptions at any time via the Settings → Export Backup (JSON) feature.
  • Deletion: You can permanently erase all stored data by clearing the app data in Android Settings (Settings → Apps → Subscription Tracker: Apex → Storage → Clear Storage) or by uninstalling the application from your device. Since we do not host your data on external servers, no remote account deletion request is required.
