Privacy Policy for 2048 Apex: Drop & Merge Puzzle

Last Updated: November 2026

This Privacy Policy informs you of our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you play 2048 Apex: Drop & Merge Puzzle (the "Application"), developed and published by Kuyu Labs.

Privacy-First Commitment: We believe in minimal, privacy-centric data handling. We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, contacts, biometric data, precise GPS locations, or personal identity profiles on our own servers. The game is designed to be fully playable offline.

1. Information Collection and Use

To ensure game stability, diagnose unexpected crashes, and maintain puzzle balance across our 9 game modes (Classic, Drop 2048, Blitz, Target Rush, Rising Lava, Clean Sweep, Zen Garden, Daily Challenge, and Adventure Mode), the Application utilizes select industry-standard developer services:

  • Crash Reporting & Stability (Google Firebase Crashlytics): We collect anonymous crash logs and diagnostic stack traces when an unexpected error occurs. This technical data contains no personally identifiable information and is used exclusively to fix bugs and improve performance on Android devices.
  • Anonymous Game Statistics: Anonymous gameplay metrics (such as completed levels, game modes played, or puzzle milestones) may be processed to calibrate difficulty and reward balance.
  • No Personal Identifiers on Our Servers: We have explicitly disabled the collection of Advertising IDs (AAID), hardware serial numbers, and personal network identity profiles for tracking or profiling purposes.
  • No Third-Party Advertising: The application does not contain third-party ad networks, advertising trackers, or data broker SDKs. The app may feature direct, non-targeted first-party banners or store links to other puzzle games developed by Kuyu Labs.
  • Local Device Storage: Your game progress, high scores, coins, unlocked board themes, and settings are stored locally on your device via secure device storage and are not transmitted to external servers.

2. In-App Purchases & Payments

The Application offers optional digital purchases (such as Supporter Tier Packs and consumable Coin Vault refills). All transactions are processed directly and securely by the official platform store (Google Play Billing).

Kuyu Labs does not collect, process, or store your credit card numbers, payment methods, or bank information. We only receive anonymized cryptographic purchase tokens and receipts from Google Play to verify transaction status and unlock your digital entitlements.

3. Data Deletion Policy

In accordance with Google Play’s Data Safety standards, users have the right to request the deletion of their data. Because 2048 Apex: Drop & Merge Puzzle does not maintain personal accounts, cloud logins, or personal databases on our own servers, there is no personally identifiable data to erase from our systems.

To completely delete all locally stored game data, high scores, coins, and cached configurations from your device, you can:

  • Open your device Settings.
  • Go to Apps > 2048 Apex.
  • Select Storage & Cache and tap Clear Storage / Clear Data.
  • Alternatively, uninstalling the Application removes all locally stored game files immediately.

4. Third-Party Service Providers

The Application integrates with trusted Google services governed by their respective privacy policies:

  • Google Play Billing: Handles in-app purchase authentication and token management (Google Privacy Policy).
  • Google Firebase Crashlytics: Collects anonymous crash diagnostic logs to maintain app reliability (Firebase Privacy Policy).

5. Children’s Privacy

Our Application is designed for a general audience and is family-friendly. Because we do not collect personal information, we do not knowingly collect, solicit, or maintain personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe any information was inadvertently transmitted, please notify us immediately so we can address it.
